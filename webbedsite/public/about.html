S.A.Q. (Sometimes Asked Questions)

Q: What's a 'lytel saffron'?

A: A lytel saffron is a small amount of saffron. It's the name of the site and what I 'publish' my games under, taken from an old recipe for mincemeat pies.

Q: Why have a website when you could instead use [social media]?

A: I like the level of control I have over the environment. I like to learn new skills, and basic HTML is a neat skill to learn. You should try it out! You may find a fun new hobby (:


Q: What's the deal with RSS?

A: RSS is a web feed protocol where I update a file and when you check it you know what's updated. I think it's an incredible way to share stuff over the internet. Rather than having a machine guess at what will keep you staring the longest, you tell your computer who you want to hear from and it just lets you know when there's news, without ads or middlemen or whatever. Really incredible stuff. I use thunderbird to aggregate my favorite RSS feeds.
